NP Membership Benefits

NP Membership is designed for eligible non-profit organizations.

  • No membership fee
  • Networking opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events
  • Connection with other organizations

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze Membership provides essential opportunities for businesses beginning their chamber journey.

  • Business networking opportunities
  • Access to selected events
  • Business directory listing
  • Access to business information and resources

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver Membership offers additional opportunities to promote and grow your business.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Enhanced business directory visibility
  • Discounted access to selected events
  • Additional promotional opportunities

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold Membership provides premium opportunities for businesses seeking greater visibility and connections.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Premium business directory visibility
  • Priority promotional opportunities
  • Access to selected premium events
  • Additional networking opportunities
